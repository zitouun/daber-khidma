GOOD QUESTIONS

Still got
questions?

Here's what we're building toward.

What is Dabber Khdma? +

A proposed freelance marketplace built around discovering projects, comparing proposals, and managing work through clear milestones.

Is the platform available yet? +

Not yet. This is a landing-page concept for a project in its design stage. Early-access registration requires backend integration before going live.

Who is it for? +

Freelancers looking for project work and clients looking for people with the right skills. We are starting from a Tunisian identity while keeping the product welcoming to a wider audience.

Will there be AI matching? +

AI-assisted matching is proposed for a later phase. It will be designed to suggest relevant opportunities, not make irreversible decisions for users.

Can I pay or get paid on the platform? +

Real payment processing is not part of the initial academic MVP. It would require a separate integration and compliance review.
